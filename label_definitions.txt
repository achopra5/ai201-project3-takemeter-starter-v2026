# TakeMeter label definitions copied from README.md, Label Taxonomy.
# One definition per label; no labeled examples or held-out answers.

technical_report: Primarily documents a completed firsthand technical experiment, build, or investigation with concrete configuration/method and observable findings that remain informative without a request for help.
help_request: Primarily seeks a concrete recommendation or solution for the author's unresolved hardware, software, or usage decision, without a substantive completed report as the main contribution.
discussion: Primarily offers an opinion, prediction, interpretation, reaction, or open-ended proposal for debate rather than a completed technical report, a concrete personal support request, or a resource introduction.
resource_share: Primarily announces, releases, links, or teaches access to a model, tool, guide, or other resource, with any benchmark claims serving the introduction or tutorial.
